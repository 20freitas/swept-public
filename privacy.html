Swept.

Privacy policy.

Short version: your screenshots never leave your iPhone.

DATA NOT COLLECTED

Swept doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There's no account, no sign-up and no analytics tracking you.

Your photos

Swept asks for access to your photo library to find your screenshots. Reading the text inside them, sorting them into categories and spotting look-alikes all happen on your device, using Apple's built-in frameworks. Screenshots, their text and the categories are never uploaded to any server.

What's stored on your iPhone

To remember which screenshots you kept and what they say, Swept saves a small index in the app's private storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Notifications

If you turn on the Sunday sweep, Swept schedules a local notification on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active; it receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipt, never your name, email or photos. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Children

Swept isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect any data from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: hello.sweptapp@gmail.com.
