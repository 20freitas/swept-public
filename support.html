I deleted a screenshot by mistake

Open the Photos app, go to Albums, then Recently Deleted. Everything Swept deletes stays there for 30 days.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Swept. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

I changed phones and lost Pro

In Swept, open Library, tap the gear, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

Swept doesn't see my screenshots

Check that Swept has full access: Settings, Privacy & Security, Photos, Swept, Full Access.
